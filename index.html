Courier

A fast, native Mac email client for Gmail and Microsoft Outlook, organized into separate Personal and Business profiles.

What it does

  • Reads, organizes and sends email for your Google and Microsoft accounts.
  • Keeps each profile, Personal or any of your businesses, completely separate: accounts, search, rules and notifications never mix.
  • Runs local rules on new mail, such as filing receipts or silencing notifications.

How it handles your data

Courier connects directly from your Mac to Google and Microsoft. There is no Courier server: your email, sign-in tokens and settings stay on your own device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Availability

Courier is a personal project and is not offered for public download.
