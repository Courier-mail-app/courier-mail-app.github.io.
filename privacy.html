Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

Courier is a desktop email application for macOS. This policy explains what data Courier accesses and what it does with it.

Data Courier accesses

When you add an account, you sign in with Google or Microsoft and grant Courier permission to access your mailbox. Courier then reads your email messages, folders and labels, and can send email, change labels, archive and move messages to the trash on your instruction.

  • Google: the Gmail API (gmail.modify scope).
  • Microsoft: Microsoft Graph (Mail.ReadWrite, Mail.Send, User.Read, offline_access).

How the data is used

Courier uses this access only to provide its email features to you: showing, searching, filtering, organizing and sending your mail. It does not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling or training machine-learning models.

Where the data is stored

Courier has no server. It connects directly from your Mac to Google and Microsoft. Copies of your messages and your settings are stored in a local database on your Mac. Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Sharing

Courier does not sell, rent, transfer or share your data with anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Courier's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Removing an account in Courier's settings deletes its cached mail and tokens from your Mac. You can also revoke Courier's access at any time from your Google Account or Microsoft account settings. Deleting the app's data folder (~/Library/Application Support/Courier) removes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through github.com/Courier-mail-app.
